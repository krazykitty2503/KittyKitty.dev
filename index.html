// 04 PUBLIC ROADMAP

Foundations first, frontier later

This sequence expresses engineering order, not fixed delivery dates. Each stage must earn the next through evidence, tests and realistic resource limits.

  1. 01

    STABILISE THE CORE

    Harden operational systems, verify recovery, and finish current acceptance gates.

  2. 02

    SHIP USEFUL TOOLS

    Move selected ideas into small, documented products with tests and clear boundaries.

  3. 03

    CONNECT THE LAYERS

    Integrate validated interfaces, infrastructure and AI without collapsing their permissions.

  4. 04

    EXPLORE THE FRONTIER

    Prototype ambitious systems work only after the foundations are reproducible and maintainable.
